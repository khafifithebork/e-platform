Age is the number that matters. A large count with a small age is a busy week;
a small count with a large age is a job that died and nobody noticed.

Two places a transcript gets stuck, and they need different people:

  PENDING    the provider was asked and never answered, or was never asked.
             An operator's problem. Check the dead-letter queue first.

  MACHINE    machine output is waiting for a human to review it.
  IN_REVIEW  Nothing is broken; somebody has to read it.

No lesson titles or ids here on purpose. A transcript belongs to a lesson, a
lesson to a course, and a course to an instructor, so naming one would put a
person's unfinished work in a mailbox nobody audits. To see which:

  python manage.py report_metrics

This alert reports and does not repair. Retrying a transcription costs money at
a provider, and deciding to spend it is not a cron job's decision.
